# References [22]–[25]: source evidence

Reference numbers match the manuscript. See `final_references.json` for bibliographic entries.

- **[22] Creager et al. 2012.** DOI: https://doi.org/10.1061/9780784412190.002. NASA primary manuscript: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20140008625/downloads/20140008625.pdf. Used for tire-dependent push-pull traction and external evidence context. Conditions differ from the Moreland fit, so it does not establish a universal multiplier or energy saving. Figure 5 extraction is documented in `../source_extraction/wheels/PROVENANCE.md`.
- **[23] Creager et al. NASA/TM-2014-218431.** Primary record: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20150000897. Figures 11–12 support the separately calibrated whole-vehicle power-number check. Later time samples are correlated within the same experiment; observed travel reduction is supplied to the predictor. The original authors' time series were not obtained.
- **[24] Creager et al. 2015.** DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jterra.2014.12.001, Journal of Terramechanics 57, 71–80. Journal counterpart of [23], not a second independent validation. Numerical reconstruction uses [23].
- **[25] Cao et al. 2023.** DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jterra.2023.08.005, Journal of Terramechanics 110, 87–99. The author-hosted full text was read. Used for author-reported flat/slope energy comparisons, with motor-controller electrical input as its measurement boundary. No plotted trials were digitized. Detailed source and limits: `../data/INDEPENDENT_ENERGY_EVIDENCE.md`.

Reference access varies. In particular, [17] retains its inherited title/author initials provisionally because the original 1961 paper was not retrieved; no pages or DOI were invented. The other audit files identify metadata-only versus inspected-full-text evidence for each original reference.
